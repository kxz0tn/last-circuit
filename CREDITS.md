# LAST//CIRCUIT — Credits

This file matches the Credits panel inside the terminal.

## App code and writing

Original to this project. The original source and prose are under the MIT license in `LICENSE`.

That MIT grant does not cover the fonts or Three.js. Those stay under the licenses named below.

## Fonts

- Orbitron — SIL Open Font License
- Share Tech Mono — SIL Open Font License

Both are requested from a Google Fonts stylesheet. If that request fails, the terminal uses the system `ui-monospace` stack.

## Library

- Three.js r170 — MIT License — loaded from `cdn.jsdelivr.net` when the page is served over HTTP and WebGL is available.

No other third-party code is included.

## Scientific figures

Public-domain science and well-known IAU-class / JPL-class round values, rewritten in original prose. This project does not copy encyclopedia pages, textbooks, or agency pages.

Moon totals for the giant planets are catalogue figures from around mid-2026. They are marked in the files as changeable. Diameters, days, and years are given as round values, not as false precision.

## Not included

- No agency insignia and no claim of agency affiliation
- Planet and ring surfaces are procedural greyscale maps drawn in the page. No downloaded imagery.
- No telescope photographs
- No stock photos, icon kits, UI kits, or paid fonts
- No copyrighted music or film lines
- No live agency API

## Lore and fact

Lines tagged LORE are framing for the terminal. Lines tagged FACT are the science. Lore never replaces a measurement.

## How this stays free

No accounts, no payments, no ads, and no analytics kits. The folder is the whole product.
